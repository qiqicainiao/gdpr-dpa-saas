2. What the service does

The tool collects structured processing facts for one customer relationship and asks a server-side generator to select clauses and annexes for a data processing agreement draft. You can review each clause, apply server-generated alternatives, copy the draft and print it with your browser. All generator features are free in this MVP.

3. No legal advice

The output is a starting draft for review. It is not legal advice, it does not create a client relationship, and it does not guarantee GDPR compliance. Have qualified counsel review the agreement before signature.

The generator does not decide whether your processing is lawful, whether a transfer mechanism applies, whether your security measures are adequate, or whether a sub-processor arrangement is permitted. It does not produce a data protection impact assessment, a record of processing activities, or a transfer impact assessment.
