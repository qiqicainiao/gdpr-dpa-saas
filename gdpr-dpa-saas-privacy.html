Privacy Policy

How this drafting tool handles the information you enter, the analytics events you can accept or reject, and the data kept in your own browser.

Last updated: 13 November 2026

1. Who operates this tool

This site provides a free drafting tool for SaaS companies that act as processors under the GDPR. The site operator controls the generation service and the analytics described below. For any privacy request, contact apj56423@gmail.com.

2. What you enter and why

The generator needs structured facts to produce a customer-specific draft. The table below describes each group of information and where it goes.

Information processed by the generator
Information Why it is processed Where it goes
Party names, addresses, countries and optional contact emails To address the draft and identify the controller and processor Sent to the generation service with the request; not written to local history or analytics
Service summary, purposes, data categories and data subject categories To select the clauses and annexes that apply to this customer Sent to the generation service; only normalized presets and counts may be kept in your browser
Sub-processor names, countries and service categories To produce the sub-processor annex and authorization terms Sent to the generation service; vendor names are excluded from analytics and local history
Transfer path, security measures, breach window and termination terms To select the transfer, security and termination clauses Sent to the generation service; normalized presets may be kept in your browser

Do not enter customer records, special-category details, identification numbers, passwords, API keys or any real personal data sample. The generator only needs categories and short descriptions.

3. Analytics and consent

Analytics are optional. The consent banner appears after your first interaction with the page, not before. Accepting and rejecting take the same number of steps, and every generator feature works if you reject.

When you accept, the tool may send events about how it is used: which page a request started from, timings, counts of purposes or clauses, which clause was expanded, whether a clause was confirmed or corrected, and whether a draft was copied or printed. These events use a per-tab reference and never include party names, addresses, email addresses, service descriptions, sub-processor names or draft text.

When you reject or have not decided, only anonymous aggregate counts are sent. No per-tab reference, timestamp, page sequence or clause detail is included.

You can withdraw consent at any time by emailing apj56423@gmail.com or by clearing site data in your browser. Withdrawal stops further analytics and does not affect the generator.

4. What is stored in your browser

The tool uses the following browser storage keys:

  • gdpr-dpa-saas:history:v1 — local workspace records: customer reference, normalized processing presets, template version and clause variants you reviewed. It never contains party names, addresses, email addresses, service descriptions, sub-processor names or document text.
  • gdpr-dpa-saas:session — a per-tab reference and generation sequence used while the tab is open.
  • gdpr-dpa-saas:consent:v1 — your analytics choice and the time it was made.
  • gdpr-dpa-saas:tab-draft — an optional draft of the current form for this tab, cleared when you reset the form or close the tab.

You can delete local workspace records from the workspace panel and clear all site data through your browser settings.

5. Legal bases

The generation request is processed to provide the draft you asked for. Analytics events are processed on the basis of your consent. Local workspace storage is used to provide the feature you selected and can be cleared at any time.

6. Retention

Form values are used to answer the generation request and are not copied into local history. Server-side analytics events are written to daily files and deleted automatically on a rolling basis after 395 days (about 13 months); de-identified aggregate counts may be kept longer. To ask about the current retention period, contact apj56423@gmail.com.

Local workspace records remain until you delete them, clear site data, or your browser reclaims storage.

7. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. The generation service and hosting infrastructure are operated for this site, and access is limited to what is needed to run and secure the tool. Contact us for details about the current hosting location and processors.

8. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of your personal data, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent. Send requests to apj56423@gmail.com. You can also complain to your local supervisory authority.

9. Children

This is a business drafting tool and is not directed to children. Do not use it to enter information about children.

10. Changes to this policy

If the tool’s data practices change, this page will be updated before the change takes effect. The date at the top of the page shows the current version.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy or the tool: apj56423@gmail.com.
